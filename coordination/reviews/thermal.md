## Actionable finding T1 — P2: reciprocal overflow silently discards heat

- Calculator: `thermal.parallel_conduction_paths`.
- Anchor: `backend/catalog/thermal.json:599` (equivalent-resistance expression); dependent branch heat expressions at lines 611 and 617. New catalog lines are patch-introduced.
- Complete accepted input vector: `path_one_length=1e-309`, `path_one_area=1`, `path_one_conductivity=1`, `path_two_length=1`, `path_two_area=1`, `path_two_conductivity=1`, `sink_temperature=300`, `source_power=1`. All supplied inputs satisfy every scalar bound and there are no cross-input constraints for this model. This is a numerical-domain counterexample, not a claim that its extreme geometry represents a realizable continuum solid.
- Independent expected behavior: Fourier conduction gives `R1=1e-309 K/W`, `R2=1 K/W`. Algebraically `Req=R1/(1+R1/R2)≈1e-309 K/W`; `Q1=P/(1+R1/R2)≈1 W`; `Q2=P*(R1/R2)/(1+R1/R2)≈1e-309 W`. The invariant is `Q1+Q2=P=1 W`. Rounded hot temperature may legitimately remain 300 K; that does not justify zero heat.
- Evidence: actual new expression is `1/(1/path_one_resistance+1/path_two_resistance)`. Engine output dispatch at `backend/src/lib.rs:98-114` evaluates the whole meval expression and checks only the final value for finiteness. **[INFERENCE: static binary64 behavior, not runtime observation]** `1/R1` overflows to positive infinity, and the enclosing reciprocal becomes finite zero. The engine consequently accepts `Req=0` and both subsequent branch heat values zero, silently violating conservation. Primary Fourier equations were inspected at University of South Carolina ECHE460 equations 1–2 and 13–14: https://cse.sc.edu/~gatzke/460/F-Heat%20Conduction.pdf .
- Correction: compute equivalent resistance with scaled algebra, e.g. `min(R1,R2)/(1+min(R1,R2)/max(R1,R2))`, and independently stable branch fractions; alternatively explicitly reject numerical domains permitting a nonfinite intermediate before outputs. Do not return a plausible zero or clamp bad arithmetic. Coordinate any shared evaluator change with Main. Update the executable contract in feature docs if expressions/domain change.
- Parent verification scenario: evaluate the complete vector and its path-swapped counterpart through public Engine and JSON-lines API. Expect finite positive Req, dominant branch approximately 1 W, other branch approximately 1e-309 W, and total heat 1 W, or an explicit documented numerical-domain rejection if that is the approved correction. Use relative/scale-sensitive checks; a generic absolute 1e-6 tolerance would hide the small resistance/branch heat. No runtime pass is claimed.
- Handoff: immediately sent to Main via agent message and incremental finding. Direct root bus/report writes were attempted but the tool rejected filesystem writes; parent persistence is required.

## All-eight independent model assessment

1. **thermal.resistor_power_derating** (`catalog:3-115`; basic): flat/linear interpolation, min/max plateau and endpoint, K differences/W units agree with specified curve. Ordered endpoints and Ta<=Tzero prevent negative denominator/out-of-curve extrapolation; actual power zero and negative overload margin remain allowed. Nominal oracle independently reduces to fraction 55/85=11/17, allowance 11/68 W, margin 11/68−0.12 W. Scenarios for Main: below/at breakpoint gives full allowance; endpoint gives zero; actual power above allowance yields negative margin; reject reversed endpoints/Ta>Tzero. Manufacturer source describes both conventional curves and mounting limitations; the model intentionally does not authorize source-discussed low-temperature uprating. No finding.
2. **thermal.junction_feedback_equilibrium** (`catalog:117-286`; expert): heat balance `Tj−Ta=R P_ref[1+beta(Tj−Tref)]` gives denominator `1−R P_ref beta`, equilibrium power `P_ref[1+beta(Ta−Tref)]/(1−g)`, and perturbation growth rate `(P_ref beta−1/R)/C`; local stability requires g<1. Guard order rejects nonpositive ambient-fitted power and g>=1 before final fit division. Ambient/reference fit checks plus positive power and stable denominator imply Tj>=fit_min; maximum-fit guard is present. K, W, 1/K and K/W consistent. Example g=4/25, rise=1000/21 K, power=50/21 W agree. Direct equilibrium-power expression avoids recomputing power from rounded absolute Tj. Scenarios: beta=0 constant-power result; accepted exact fit endpoints; reject g=1/g>1, nonpositive ambient fitted power, out-of-fit Ta/Tref and equilibrium exceeding fit_max. Near-unity physical sensitivity is explicitly documented, not falsely certified. No finding.
3. **thermal.arrhenius_acceleration** (`catalog:288-390`; expert): inspected NIST AF expression has correct use/test sign, eV/K Boltzmann constant and failure-site absolute K. Rewritten temperature difference is algebraically identical and avoids reciprocal cancellation; test>=use, positive Ea/T and duration>=0 are guarded. Logarithmic bound includes duration so factor and equivalent exposure remain computationally bounded. Example exponent 4.352152076475486, AF77.64538204961666 and seconds27952337.537862 agree with the independent exponential derivation in research; not a runtime-oracle claim. Scenarios: equal temperatures AF=1; zero duration exposure=0; reject reversed temperatures and exponent exceeding duration-dependent bound; test near-equal temperatures, including AF rounding to unity at sub-ulp increments. Caveats correctly restrict mechanism, stresses and meaning (not MTBF/life/confidence). No finding.
4. **thermal.heatsink_resistance_budget** (`catalog:392-487`; intermediate): dominant-case-face series chain and subtraction of package/interface allowance correct. Nonpositive sink budget is deliberately rejected per backlog, not a suppressed diagnostic margin. Positive power/junction-case resistance, nonnegative interface and ceiling>Ta correct. Nominal budget 80/10=8 K/W and 8−1.5−0.5=6 K/W. Scenarios: zero interface; reject zero power, ceiling<=Ta and exhausted sink headroom; substitute selected sink bound to recover ceiling. Inspected TI evidence supports single-face caveat and airflow dependence. No finding.
5. **thermal.parallel_conduction_paths** (`catalog:489-640`; intermediate): constant-area Fourier geometry, common isothermal endpoints and conductance addition correct over ordinary domain; lengths m/areas m²/k W/(m K), thermal resistance K/W and heat W consistent. Nominal R1=1/4, R2=5, Req=5/21, rise5K, heat20+1W; source power zero remains permitted. No spreading/PCB simulation claim. **Finding T1** identifies failure of numerical conservation despite allowed finite inputs. Additional Main scenarios: branch swap, equal paths half-power each, source=0, small heat flow on large absolute-temperature offset, reject every nonpositive geometry/conductivity.
6. **thermal.lumped_thermal_step** (`catalog:642-833`; intermediate): independent energy balance `rho V cp dT/dt=P−hAs(T−Ta)` confirms Cth=rho V cp, R=1/(hAs), tau=RC and Bi=hV/(As k). V/As convention and Bi<0.1 directly supported by both university sources. Adding uniform source P is explicit derivation. Complementary exp/−expm1 weights give T(0)=Tinitial, cooling when P=0 and T∞ at long times while retaining small heating weights. Nominal C=24.3 J/K,R=10 K/W,tau243s,Bi5e-5,T∞318.15K,T(tau)=318.15−20/e. Scenarios: t=0; short t/tau; long time; initial below/above steady; P=0; reject Bi>=0.1/nonpositive heat/geometry properties. Small Bi is not claimed proof of uniform localized junction heating. No finding.
7. **thermal.resistor_tcr_drift** (`catalog:835-962`; basic): signed ppm/K conversion 1e-6, local constant-component-TCR model and positive final-resistance factor correct; both temperatures must be inside ordered validated fit interval. No division by deltaT, so zero temperature difference/TCR valid. Nominal drift0.006, change60ohm,resistance10060ohm; negative100ppm/K at the same example temperatures instead gives drift−0.006, change−60ohm,resistance9940ohm. Scenarios: zero/signed coefficient, cooling, reject factor<=0 and fit interval/temperature violations. Datasheet ±TCR is documented as bounds rather than a known positive slope; self-heating and component-versus-element TCR caveats match inspected source. No finding.
8. **thermal.free_linear_expansion** (`catalog:964-1100`; basic): free directional mean engineering coefficient gives strain alpha deltaT, change L0 strain and final L0+change. m/K/1/K units, signed cooling/negative CTE and fit guards correct. Declared first-order approximation for differential coefficient is honest; absolute strain<=0.01 guarantees positive final length. Nominal strain0.00184, deltaL0.000184m, final0.100184m; reversing temperatures changes deltaL sign. Scenarios: deltaT=0/alpha=0, signed coefficients/cooling, exact ±1% boundary, reject larger strain/out-of-fit/reversed fit. Source defines directionality and temperature dependence, not an aluminum coefficient; docs correctly label example illustrative. No finding.

## Consuming-side integration trace

Inspected `backend/build.rs`: sorted `.json` catalog files are embedded via include_str into CATALOGS. `Engine::new` deserializes every lane, indexes all calculator IDs and parses outputs/constraints; there is no thermal-specific filter. `Engine::calculate` looks up by ID, validates exact finite input keys/ranges, routes all constraint operators explicitly, then evaluates every output sequentially and publishes each in the result map. `respond` handles Calculate/Catalog; main reads each JSON line and emits serialized response. Native expm1 is registered in shared context, covering the new lumped expression. No introduced thermal output is silently dropped by backend dispatch; T1 is arithmetic inside that path. Frontend/platform behavior was not executed or certified.
