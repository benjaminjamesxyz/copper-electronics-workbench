## Actionable finding DIODE-R1 — P2

**Calculator:** `diodes.parallel_led_ballast`.
**Location:** `backend/catalog/diodes.json:1086–1089` (`current_two` expression at 1089); corresponding expression and conditioning explanation in `docs/features/diodes.md:367–378`.
**Title:** Compute the smaller LED branch without subtracting the rounded larger branch.

Accepted input vector: `total_current=0.5000000000000001`, `forward_one=0.5`, `forward_two=1`, `allowed_difference=0.5`, `ballast_resistance=1`, `supply=5`, `sink_headroom_min=0.3`. Every scalar range and cross-input guard passes: allowed_difference < total_current, selected R equals mismatch/allowed_difference, mismatch < total_current*R, and supply exceeds 1 V branch voltage plus 0.3 V headroom.

Independent expected result: KCL and equal branch voltages give `I2=(Itotal-(VF2-VF1)/R)/2`. Here Itotal is exactly `1/2+2^-53`, mismatch/R is exactly `1/2`, and the smaller branch must be `2^-54 = 5.551115123125783e-17 A`. The current implementation first evaluates I1=(Itotal+1/2)/2. The sum `1+2^-53` is exactly halfway between adjacent binary64 values at 1 and rounds to 1 by round-to-nearest-even. I1 therefore rounds to 1/2. Subtracting that from Itotal yields `2^-53 = 1.1102230246251565e-16 A` for I2, a 100% error. The small result is representable; this is avoidable subtractive conditioning, not ordinary underflow or an invalid physical domain. The preceding direct mismatch computation for current_difference correctly addresses a different cancellation, but does not protect current_two.

Evidence: inspected catalog, sequential f64 evaluation in `backend/src/lib.rs:84–122`, TI SLVA325 pages 2–5 (KCL and equal branch voltage), and exact binary64 round-to-even arithmetic. **Static prediction, not an executed engine failure.** Independent model arithmetic is not a real-device characterization claim.

Correction: calculate both branch currents directly from signed mismatch, e.g. `current_two=(total_current+(forward_one-forward_two)/ballast_resistance)/2`; do not reconstruct the small branch from an already rounded large branch. Keep existing positivity, matching and sink-compliance domains; update affected feature-doc expression/conditioning discussion without weakening tolerances or excluding near-extinction inputs.

Parent verification: evaluate the above full vector and a forward_one/forward_two swap. Require the small branch to equal `2^-54` with relative or specifically justified scale-sensitive tolerance (a blanket absolute 1e-6 would hide the defect). Retain the nominal complete example, equal-Vf sharing, KCL and existing rejection regressions. No implementation changes made by reviewer.

## All-eight model assessment

1. **diodes.led_series_resistor** (`backend/catalog/diodes.json:3–153`): KVL gives R=(Vs−N Vf)/I. Nominal resistor/LED powers sum to supplied electrical power; maximum-current and resistor-power corners use maximum supply and minimum Vf. Integer N and positive nominal headroom are enforced; bound ordering guarantees positive worst headroom. All five example outputs agree with independent substitution (150 ohm, 0.06 W, 0.04 W, 3.7/150 A, 13.69/150 W). Constant-Vf, excluded resistor tolerance, separate preferred-value/derating choice and electrical-versus-optical power caveats are honest. Kingbright page 5 explicitly supports individual ballast resistors and temperature-aware current selection. No actionable defect found.

2. **diodes.shockley_forward_current** (`backend/catalog/diodes.json:155–285`): VT=kT/q, current=Is*expm1(VD/(nVT)), and rd=nVT/(ID+Is) follow Shockley law and its derivative at fixed T. Positive Is/T/n bound the denominator; zero VD gives zero current/power and finite rd. Exponent<=40 and fitted-current ceiling bound the large-argument range. Native expm1 is registered and used in both current and constraint, preserving tiny positive bias relative to direct exp−1. At 300 K VT≈25.852 mV; default exponent≈23.209 and current≈12.0104 mA are consistent with the complete worked oracle. Temperature-dependent Is fitting, omitted series resistance/high injection/breakdown/self-heating and non-rating ceiling are explicit. Auburn and the lower-weight Georgia Tech Diodes subsection were directly inspected. No actionable defect found.

3. **diodes.zener_shunt_resistor_window** (`backend/catalog/diodes.json:287–499`): KCL IZ=(Vin−Vz)/Ractual−IL gives correct min/max nominal-R bounds for Ractual=R(1±t). Ordered envelopes, input headroom, positive regulation currents and a nonempty selected-R interval guard all divisions. Default actual R=370.5–409.5 ohm gives low/high Iz≈6.84982/27.26046 mA. Vz,max*Iz,high is deliberately a conservative independent-corner bound, not falsely labeled simultaneous exact maximum. Resistor power uses the simultaneous largest drop/smallest R corner. TI SLVAG00 section 2.2 and 6.1.1 directly support dynamic-voltage/temperature caveats and the KCL/power analysis; tolerance extensions are derived transparently. No actionable defect established.

4. **diodes.reverse_recovery_charge_budget** (`backend/catalog/diodes.json:501–616`): Triangle area is Ipeak*tbase/2 independent of peak position; bus-voltage charge transport is a defined cell contribution VR*Q, repeated at entered event frequency. Occupancy<=0.1 and positive duration/frequency prevent overlapping modeled events; zero peak/voltage behave algebraically correctly. Default 200 nC, 80 microjoules, 8 W and 20 mA follow directly from triangle integration. Full zero-to-zero fitted base is clearly distinguished from percentage-threshold datasheet trr; onsemi page 9 directly confirms its 10% endpoint and charge-current integral. No diode-only heat, total switching energy or automatic hardware safety is claimed. ST AN5028 remained inaccessible in this review, so its detailed cell-energy derivation is not independently primary-source verified. No actionable defect found within the stated budget model.

5. **diodes.bridge_conduction_loss** (`backend/catalog/diodes.json:618–748`): Time-average two conducting junctions, 2(V0*i+rd*i²), to get 2(V0*Iavg+rd*Irms²). Four equal-loss junctions under the explicit symmetry assumption each get one quarter; whole-period rectified-path RMS is correctly distinguished from per-diode/on-time RMS. Guards enforce average<=RMS<=peak and RMS²<=peak*average. Defaults produce 1.76 W, 0.44 W/junction and 1.88 V peak conducting-path drop. Zero-current characteristic intercept is explicitly not predicted open-circuit terminal voltage. Vishay bridge datasheet directly confirms four junctions/per-diode Vf and separate capacitive-load ratings. Affine-loss note could not be extracted, so the averaging result is independently derived from the declared model, not claimed as a source quote. No actionable defect found.

6. **diodes.full_wave_capacitor_ripple** (`backend/catalog/diodes.json:750–856`): Full bridge doubles line recharge frequency; available capacitor peak subtracts two diode drops from sqrt(2)*source RMS. ΔQ=IL/(2fline) and ΔV=ΔQ/C give ripple; mean/trough and AC RMS about mean correctly use the stated sawtooth approximation (RMS=Vpp/sqrt(12), not Vpp/sqrt(2)). Positive frequency/C, source headroom and <=10% ripple constrain model; zero load gives zero ripple and equal peak/trough/DC. Default charge loss 1 mC gives 0.454545 Vpp; all six output oracles follow independent substitution. Purdue equation 2.5 and Auburn full-wave interval directly inspected. Finite conduction angle, source impedance, ESR and charging/surge current limits are openly excluded; 10% is a modeling restriction, not standards compliance. No actionable defect found.

7. **diodes.biased_two_sided_clipper** (`backend/catalog/diodes.json:858–996`): Inspected diode orientation gives upper level Vref,H+Vf,H and lower Vref,L−Vf,L. Ordered references/nonnegative drops imply ordered clipping levels; min(max()) correctly represents lower/interior/upper regions without clamping invalid inputs. Signed input/references are permitted and clamp currents are positive magnitudes with the stated directions. Default upper case gives 3.95 V, 4.05 mA and 0.0164025 W; independent lower case Vi=−3 V at default other inputs gives −1.65 V, lower current 1.35 mA, upper current zero and resistor power 0.0018225 W; interior Vi=0 gives unchanged zero voltage and zero currents/power. Northwestern prelab constant-drop model and clipper text were directly inspected; figure topology is independently established by the catalog's explicit anode/cathode assumptions, not reconstructed from unavailable text-only diagrams. Stiff source/high-impedance/quasistatic, breakdown and ESD caveats are honest. No actionable defect found.

8. **diodes.parallel_led_ballast** (`backend/catalog/diodes.json:998–1163`): Two separate equal ballasts feeding one common total-current sink correctly differ from voltage-fed series design. Equal branch voltage plus KCL yields the stated branch equations, Rmin and mismatch/R; the latter direct computation is a good promoted conditioning correction. Compliance guard and positive branches are physically consistent. Defaults: Rmin=50 ohm, 20 mA±(0.2/136 A), branch drop=4.46 V, combined power=0.0546941 W, headroom=0.54 V. Equal-Vf reduces to equal currents, zero mismatch/Rmin and finite selected ballast. **DIODE-R1 above remains actionable near extinction.** No additional topology/unit finding established.

## Cross-boundary integration inspected

`backend/build.rs` includes all `.json` catalog files, without a per-lane allowlist. `Engine::new` loads each lane and builds an ID map; `Engine::calculate` validates exact input keys/ranges and input-only constraints, then sequentially evaluates every declared output into a result map. The `expm1` function introduced for Shockley is explicitly registered to `f64::exp_m1`. `Request::Calculate`/`respond` routes ID and input map to the same generic evaluator; main's line loop returns the response. Flutter `Calculator` generically deserializes inputs/outputs/example metadata, backend client accepts success/error responses generically, and UI maps every declared output key. No diode ID, extra output or function is silently dropped in inspected dispatch points. This is source inspection, not runtime integration verification.
