- [TI SPRAAV2A](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/spraav2a/spraav2a.pdf), sections 6/6.1/6.3: inspected directly; round-aperture scope, opening/wall definition and >=0.66 guideline. Rectangle formula assessed by independent geometry, not claimed copied from source.
- [JLCPCB copper guide](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/jlcpcb-copper-weight): inspected directly; explicit 35 µm/oz designation and supplier-capability caveats.
- [Eurocircuits IAR](https://www.eurocircuits.com/iar-inner-annular-ring/): inspected directly; production tool formula, supplier allowance and off-centre hole discussion. Offset subtraction assessed independently by circle geometry.
- [TI SLUP224](https://www.ti.com/lit/pdf/slup224), Topic 4 section II, Table 1 and Figure 3: inspected directly; rho*L/A, bulk 1.70 µohm·cm, plated 6.0 µohm·cm and annular via geometry. Historical illustrative process values are not modern universal resistivity data.
- [Michigan Tech thermoelectromagnetics](https://multiphysics.geo.mtu.edu/thermoelectromagnetics.html), Joule Heating: inspected directly; local J·E and rho|J|² power density.
- [TI SNAA103](https://www.ti.com/lit/pdf/snaa103), Chapter 6 Minimizing Skew: inspected; same-environment path mismatch and 6.84 ps/mm illustrative propagation scale.
- [NIST HB133 Appendix E](https://www.nist.gov/document/2026-hb-133-appendix-e), length tables: inspected directly; international inch/foot conversions supporting mil conversion.
- IPC-2152 controlling standard and TI SPRACP4A were not freshly inspected during this review. The preserved public-scope/access caveat is not upgraded to full-standard validation. No material formula uncertainty depended on those uninspected references.
