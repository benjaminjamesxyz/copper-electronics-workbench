## Research boundary — transients
- To integration/timing/data/magnetics/power/filters: transients owns general RC and RL step trajectories (time targets included, not counted separately), periodic switched-source RC extrema, ideal capacitor constant-current ramps, capacitor charge sharing, RC ramp-source response, an unloaded two-stage passive RC ladder step, and underdamped series-RLC step overshoot.
