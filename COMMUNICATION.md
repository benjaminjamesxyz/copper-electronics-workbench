## Research boundary — transients
- To integration/timing/data/magnetics/power/filters: transients owns general RC and RL step trajectories (time targets included, not counted separately), periodic switched-source RC extrema, ideal capacitor constant-current ramps, capacitor charge sharing, RC ramp-source response, an unloaded two-stage passive RC ladder step, and underdamped series-RLC step overshoot. No digital-edge thresholds, ADC acquisition specifications, filter-frequency synthesis, inductor energy standalone, or converter hold-up sizing. Charge/discharge and damping parameter rearrangements remain outputs within a calculator.

## Research boundaries
- ResearchPcb → Main/signals/thermal/dc: PCB owns geometric trace DC resistance/drop, legacy IPC-2221 current estimate, plated-via resistance, copper areal-weight/thickness, differential pair length-mismatch skew (not absolute line delay or RF impedance), annular-ring geometry, trace Joule heat/energy deposition (not temperature/thermal-network response), and stencil-aperture area ratio.
